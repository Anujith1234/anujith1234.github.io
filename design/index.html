Design view

Player and learner experience

The same five projects, ordered for design readers. Each summary focuses on what the player or learner does, what the game tells them back, and what I would change.

Also available: Programming view

  1. 01 · Unity · Web training simulation · AMMACHI Labs · 2026

    CPR Training Simulation

    The same scenario taught three ways: step-by-step guidance that blocks the wrong task but allows meaningful mistakes, an unguided timed assessment with a debrief, and a metronome practice mode for rhythm.

    Read about the experience →

  2. 02 · Unity · Online multiplayer · RoA Interactive · 2026

    Move Your Butt

    A reversal of 'hole in the wall': players make the holes for each other. Food, fuel and cannon volleys put pressure on the passing team while the wall speeds up over twenty rounds.

    Read about the experience →

  3. 03 · Unity · VR training · AMMACHI Labs · Sole developer

    Rooftop Solar Connection Simulator

    A full job told in narrated steps: dress safely, test before you connect, and see what a wrong connection does, with a retry loop that puts the cables back so the learner can try again.

    Read about the experience →

  4. 04 · Unity · Hardware-linked trainer · AMMACHI Labs · 2026 modernization

    Bar Bending Simulator

    Physical practice with immediate on-screen guidance: target markers and colour cues for each bend, a return-to-zero step between bends, and reports that let instructors compare a trainee with an expert.

    Read about the experience →

  5. 05 · Unreal Engine 5 · Prototype, paused · RoA Interactive · 2025–2026

    Madan: The Hope

    The groundwork for a driving game built around small physical jobs: look at something to see what it is, hold to act on it, carry parts, and climb into the bus to drive.

    Read about the experience →
