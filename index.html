About

Game designer and developer · Kollam, Kerala, India

I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. I joined AMMACHI Labs as a game design intern, writing storyboards, interaction flows, instructional sequences and voice-over scripts for vocational training, and stayed on as a game developer and designer.

That mix shapes how I work. I design what the player or learner does and what the game tells them back, then build and debug the systems that deliver it, with designers, artists, developers and subject-matter experts.

Alongside my role at AMMACHI Labs, I develop games with RoA Interactive, an independent collaborative team: Move Your Butt, an online multiplayer party game in Unity, and Madan: The Hope, an Unreal Engine prototype.

I care about feedback the player can read at a glance, rules that can be tested outside the engine, and keeping older projects working on current tools.
